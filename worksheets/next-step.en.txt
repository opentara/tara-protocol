TARA Protocol — Start with a situation you actually face.
In development · Conceptual overview · 21 October 2026

We do not need to know everything or agree on everything to act together. We need to know which next step is acceptable, who may take it, within what limits, and when to reconsider.

1. Participants & effects
Who acts, who decides, and who bears the consequences? Who is missing from the conversation?

2. Interests & differences

3. Knowledge & unknowns

4. A sufficient next step

5. Authority & resources
What is already permitted? Who must agree to a change? What time, attention and other resources can we spend?

6. Stop & review
What triggers a pause? When will we review the effects, who participates, and how can the agreement change?
